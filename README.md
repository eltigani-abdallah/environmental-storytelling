# Environmental Storytelling

A wordless narrative experience built as a student project at LaPlateforme_ Marseille, telling a story entirely through environmental design and atmosphere: no dialogue, no text (almost).

> You're not supposed to read the synopsis before playing. Everything should be easy enough to figure out on your own.

![enviro-storytelling](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f9a94c61-91e7-47eb-b45c-9e3ddd4e7b0c)

## Installation

1. Go to the [**Releases**](../../releases) tab and download `EnvironmentalStorytelling.zip`
2. Unzip the file
3. Open the folder matching your OS, the game is built for **Windows** and **Linux**
4. Run the game:
   - **Linux:** double-click the file ending in `x86_64`
   - **Windows:** double-click the `.exe` file

## Synopsis

You get out of your car one night only to find the pavement leading to your door covered in a red trail. Is it blood? You ask yourself as you walk into your house to investigate, like the white person in a horror movie you are.

## Scene by Scene

### Scene 1: Leaving the Car
*Beat: suspense, curiosity*

You leave the car and look down to find a red trail extending all the way to your front door. Your shoes make sloshing sounds in the liquid as you approach. (BGM: *Premonition* - Undertale)

### Scene 2: The Never-Ending Hallway
*Beat: suspense, horror*

The trail continues into the house. You turn on your phone flashlight for visibility, and to not attract attention (never mind that you came through the front door). (BGM: *But Nobody Came* - Undertale)

### Scene 3: All Shall Be Revealed
*Beat: relief*

You walk into the living room to find your work colleague holding a birthday cake. The red splashes on the floor match the ones outside. You look up to see the banner they were trying to paint celebrating your birthday. The end. (BGM: *Home* - Undertale)
