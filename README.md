


# Highlights

- A wordless narrative experience built as a student project
- A story told entirely through environmental design and atmosphere

# Overview

A student project created at LaPlateforme_ Marseille to test the student's ability to tell a story without using words. (A small exception was made for something added in the very last scene.)

As such, you, dear reader, are not supposed to read the synopsis and scene by scene breakdown before completing the game. Everything should be easy enough to figure out on your own.

# Installation

- Step 1: Go to the release section from the side and download `EnvironmentalStorytelling.zip`

- Step 2: Unzip the file

- Step 3: Navigate to the folder with the title of your OS. This game is currently built for both Windows and Linux

- Step 4: Run the game. On Linux, simply double click the file ending in `x86_64`. On Windows, double click the `.exe` file

### Synopsis

You get out of your car one night only to find the pavement leading to your door covered in a red trail. Is it blood? You ask yourself as you walk into your house to investigate, like the white person in a horror movie you are.

### Scene by Scene

#### Scene 1: Leaving the Car

- Beat: suspense, curiosity

You leave the car and look down only to find the red trail extending all the way to your front door. You walk further as your shoes make sloshing sounds in the liquid, making your way towards your front door. (BGM: Premonition from Undertale)

#### Scene 2: The Never-Ending Hallway

- Beat: suspense, horror

You enter your house only to find that the trail extends all the way to your living room. You turn on your phone flashlight for visibility and to not attract attention to yourself (even though it was dumb enough of you to enter through the front door, which is audible throughout the house). You hear footsteps other than yours coming from the living room. (BGM: But Nobody Came from Undertale)

#### Scene 3: All Shall Be Revealed

- Beat: relief

You walk into the living room only to find your work colleague holding a birthday cake. The floor is covered in red splashes, the same ones from the pavement outside and the hallway. You look up to see the banner they were trying to paint, celebrating your birthday. The end. (BGM: Home from Undertale)
